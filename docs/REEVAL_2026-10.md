# Model re-evaluation, October 2026

Owner request (2026-10-25): with the new stats now collected (tracking, potential assists,
starters, shot quality, matchups for the current season), re-evaluate whether the player
projections and lineups can be improved. One idea, in the owner's own words: "how well a
player does against certain teams… might help determine how good or bad they will play against
that team." Treat each idea as a **hypothesis**, run it through the existing walk-forward
harness, and bring the owner a results table. Nothing ships without his approval.

Background reading: `docs/models-explained.html` (the full chain; Appendix B lists where the
docs have drifted from the code), `docs/PLAYERS.md` §4, §7 and Phase 2F (earlier ablations F1–F6),
`docs/HANDOFF.md`, and `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`.

## Protocol (same as every earlier ablation)
- Walk-forward: every fit uses only seasons < S, and features use only data from before the game.
  Each new feature group gets leak tests: scramble/truncate after the cut, so the features up to
  the cut stay identical.
- Bar: NB log-lik per row and DK CRPS/MAE per minute, clustered SE, on 2023-24, 2024-25 and
  2025-26. Then end to end in the T2.1 harness (avail protocol, closing lines, common random
  numbers, paired against the live arm). Then the T2.4 live-like slice. **A group ships only if it
  clears > 1 SE on CRPS end to end and is not worse on the live slice.**
- Every change goes behind a `*_DEFAULT = False` switch with an env override, and must be
  bit-identical when OFF.
- **Compute:**
  - Heavy runs go only in Mac scratch `~/Developer/courtside-compute/<name>/`, with `nice -n 10`
    and at most 1 heavy process per workstream.
  - Never touch the prod DB or `var/`; copy what you need.
  - No heavy Mac runs from 12:00 to 12:50 ET or from 15:45 to 19:45 ET (lineup and lock builds).
  - On 2026-10-25 the Mac is heavily loaded by other projects, so heavy runs wait for the
    orchestrator's go-ahead; build and validate on small samples first.

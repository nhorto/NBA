How Courtside makes its predictions

Technical explainer for the owner. Last updated 2026-10-25. Every switch state below was read from the *_DEFAULT constants in code, not from a doc.

In one paragraph: a stacked LightGBM game model prices margin and total (with and without the market line). On the player side, a chain of separately fitted models produces, per player, P(play), a minutes distribution conditional on the game margin, shrunk per-minute rates with a learned context multiplier, and a usage bump when a star sits. A Path A simulator draws the game environment, availability scenarios and minutes, then per-stat count outcomes through a Gaussian copula; points are reconciled to the book team totals. Two post-draw corrections follow: a role-aware blend toward the last-10 average and an upper-tail remap. The sims feed a CP-SAT optimizer (cash, Top N), a contest builder scored against a modelled field, the X factor, late swap and a props comparison. Everything is fit walk-forward on seasons before the test season, and every change ships behind an environment switch with an OFF-is-bit-identical proof and the owner's sign-off.

2. Game model (win probability, margin, total)

docs/MODEL.md (rungs 1–3), docs/MODEL_CARD.md (2026-10-19), docs/research/game-calibration.md, src/courtside/model/gbm.py, model/features.py, model/players.py, injuries.py

Production is "rung 3": a linear base plus a LightGBM residual, one model per target (margin, total), with P(home win) derived from the margin. Two production specs exist: gbm_market (uses the line; it drives the Brief's headline numbers and the player sims' fallback environment) and gbm_opinion (no market inputs; "our number").

Inputs: the player-strength engine and Elo

  • Data. team_games (stats.nba.com league game log) 2000-01 → 2025-26, 33,447 games after validation; 2000-01 → 2002-03 are burn-in. Neutral sites, the bubble and franchise relocations are curated (model/sites.py, teams.franchise).
  • Player engine (rung 2). Ridge APM with a box-score prior, one causal pass day by day. Per player-game the on-court plus-minus has expectation (mi/48)·(ri + Σteammates rk·4mk/(5G − mi) − Σopp rj·mj/G + h), weight 48/mi. Posterior mean solves (A + λI) r = b + λμ with A, b accumulated with exponential decay. Box prior μ = β·(z − z̄), z = shrunk per-100 rates (600 pseudo-possessions) of 11 box stats plus minutes, evidence and rookie / sophomore flags; β refit each season by a predictive regression on the previous 6 seasons.
  • Frozen engine hyperparameters (tuned on 2000-01 → 2010-11 only): λ = 300, APM half-life 1,460 d, summer carry 0.8, box half-life 365 d, box carry 0.6, box n0 = 600, minutes EWMA α 0.2 / 0.02.
  • Elo: K = 20, HCA = 90, summer carry 0.65, MOV multiplier (|MOV|+3)0.8/(7.5 + 0.006·Elodiff,winner); frozen on 2000-11.
  • Availability. Live: latest injury snapshot per player mapped by the hand-set injuries.PLAY_PROB (Out 0, Doubtful 0.10, Questionable 0.5, Day-to-day 0.6, Probable 0.9, Available 1). The player side's calibrated cells are not used here yet (IMPROVEMENTS B4). Backtest: avail_tight, the leak-safe oracle: regulars (projected ≥ 20 min) by who played, fringe players assumed available. The full "who played" oracle leaks game flow (56.5% ATS artefact) and is never used.

How the market enters: gbm_market vs gbm_opinion

  • gbm_market carries the spread and total both in the linear base and as tree features. Training uses closing lines; live it is priced on the newest captured pre-tip line (pregame odds are captured in-run, about 1 minute old; IMPROVEMENTS G1). Fed the open instead of the close, it loses 0.008 LL on 879 2025-26 games, so early runs are noisier than the backtest.
  • gbm_opinion has no market input. It is used for "our number vs the market" and its departures from the close are mostly noise (bdisagree 0.11, SE 0.05: about 11% of a departure is borne out).
  • For player sims, the market enters primarily through the book team totals (§3.6), not through the game model.
  • Built OFF: +nsl news-since-line shift (−0.0009 LL, 1.6 SE, post hoc only), +as absence scale (absence values are not 2× the market's: outcomes scale 1.29 ± 0.17, close 0.92 ± 0.04), a shrink-toward-close layer (−0.00089 ± 0.00039 LL, but 2024-25 +1.06 SE: misses the per-season rule; revisit after 2026-27).

3. Player projection chain

src/courtside/proj/pipeline.py, PLAYERS.md §4 and Phase 2 / 2F result blocks, IMPROVEMENTS.md §2

Order inside one projection run (pipeline.project_games → build_games → copula.simulate_game_v1 → run_games): P(play) per player → availability scenarios per team → minutes per scenario → EB rates × context multiplier (stars picked on EB rates) → usage bump → per-sim environment and minutes → per-stat counts via the copula → reconciliation of points to the implied totals → role blend → tail remap → summaries, stored sims. All parameters live in the bundle and are fit on seasons < S (S = the season of the day).

His non-play is drawn as a scenario absence, so teammates are re-projected. (2) Fringe DK-pool players (not in the team's last 10 games, not on the report) get a logistic P(plays | available): mid-season features avg10, no-appearance flag (fit on the slice's 868 fringe rows, now refit leave-one-month-out); in a team's first 5 games, log appearances in 365 days and log(DK salary / 3000) plus a minimum-salary flag, fit on RotoGuru pools 2017-21 reweighted to the not-listed population. Players DK prices ≥ $4,500 keep the minutes engine's value in that early regime. Clamp [0.02, 0.97].

Fit on 2025-26 lock−10 rows.
  • Scenarios: independent Bernoulli(P(available)) per player; per team at most 16 distinct absent sets (most frequent until 95% of sims are covered, plus "everyone uncertain plays"). Rarer sets collapse onto the kept subset with the most absentees; marginal P(play) stays exact.

With +p2: listed P(play) 0.62 → 0.71 vs 0.71 played, fringe 0.66 → 0.25 vs 0.25; DK CRPS −0.073 (two-way z −4.68, out-of-sample fringe) at lock−45, −0.076 (z −4.96) at lock−10, every tier negative. +pq: lock−10 listed P(play) LL −3.2 SE walk-forward (−4.1 leave-month-out), DFS CRPS −2.7 SE on the lock−10 slice, no tier worse, lock−45 identical. +gd (rows keyed by game date, B8) is neutral (LL −0.0005 to −0.0012) and runs as a "not worse" shadow.

Stage 2: promotion, P(plays), reconciliation, margin

  • Value-play promotion. Per absent expected starter j, a softmax conditional logit gives each available non-starter u P(u replaces j) (same / secondary / adjacent group, EWMA, starts, his shrunk historical share of j's minutes when j sat); expected lift = P × (target − base)+, target = 0.5·team starter minutes for j's group + 0.5·own EWMA. Leftover kadd of j's minutes is spread by group. The literal "top of group" rule is the real replacement only ~49% of the time.
  • P(plays | available): a logit on the state plus depth rank among available players. Under +mf it also reads the GBM's plan for the night (log plan, plan / EWMAs / median of 5) and depth-rank dummies by plan, fit on out-of-fold plans. (A LightGBM classifier was dropped: it learns the oracle's "available regulars always play".)
  • Team total: 240 (+25 per OT) minus a reserve for players outside the candidate set. Reconciliation moves each player's expected minutes in proportion to Vari ∝ p·a·(0.2² + 1 − p), capped at 40 (+5 / OT); this beat proportional water-filling by ~0.1 min MAE on every season. Under +rx, when the candidates hold the DK pool the reserve is only career debutants' expected minutes by team games into the season (~14 min at game 1, ~0.15 from game 15).
  • Margin curve: minutes(m) = mean + gtier(|m|, won/lost) − ctier, g = mean residual by 5 tiers (edges 12 / 20 / 26 / 32 min) × |margin| bins (0, 5, 10, …, 30+) × result, fit on seasons < S; evaluated at each sim's drawn margin.

Noise

mins = level × exp(μtier + c + σtier·s(ξ)·ξ),   s(ξ) = slo if ξ < 0 else shi two-piece normal in log space (+ms); c re-centres E[exp(noise)]
  • Early exit with probability q0 + q1·PF/36, scaled by U(0.35, 0.85); DNP-CD with probability 1 − p; then a per-sim proportional fill to the team total.
  • +mf: noise fit net of the margin curve (the old fit on log(actual/plan) double-counted the margin and moved ~0.6 min/game from stars to bench), then μ / σ per tier recalibrated by simulation so that played sims match actual minutes on average and the 10–90% interval covers 80%.
  • +ms: slo = (q50 − q10)/z0.9, shi = (q90 − q50)/z0.9 from three LightGBM quantile models (objective quantile; lr 0.05, 15 leaves, min leaf 400, L2 5, 300 rounds) of log(actual / (plan + g(margin))), on the GBM's features plus log plan and plan vs EWMAs, rows played with plan ≥ 5; each divided by its tier's mean and clipped to [0.4, 2.5]; μ / σ recalibrated by simulation.

3.3 Per-minute rates: empirical Bayes state

src/courtside/proj/state.py (fit_params, KAPPA_GRID, H_GRID, RHO_GRID); local var/proj/bundle_2026-27_v1.json; PLAYERS.md §4.3

For each player and stat in {pts, fg3m, reb, oreb, dreb, ast, stl, blk, tov, fga, fta, pf} (plus derived DK/min, no bonuses), as of day d:

r̂ = (Σg wg xg + κ μ) / (Σg wg ming + κ),    wg = 2−(d − dayg)/H · ρ(seasond − seasong)
  • κ is in pseudo-minutes. H (calendar-day half-life, so the summer decays by itself), ρ (extra discount per season boundary) and κ are chosen per stat on grids (H ∈ {30 … 720} d, ρ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.85, 1.0}, κ ∈ {15 … 2,600}) by maximum NB log-likelihood of the next game's count given its actual minutes, on seasons < S.
  • Prior μ: the season-to-date league rate of the player's cell = position group (G / F / C / unknown) × tier of his own decayed minutes per game (edges 12 / 20 / 26 / 32), each game using its pre-game tier, blended with last season's final cell rate (m0 = 1,500 pseudo-minutes).
  • Expected count given minutes: rate × minutes × fstat(min / mpg), a piecewise-linear table on ratio edges 0.4 … 1.5 (short nights cost efficiency).
  • Dispersion: NB φ per (stat, minutes tier), Var = μ + φμ².
  • Regular season, play-in and playoffs all feed the state. One vectorised pass; query rows are sorted before the same-day real row, so a state can never see its own day.

3.4 Usage redistribution tier bump live +us shadow

src/courtside/proj/state.py (fit_usage_bump), proj/usage.py, pipeline.py module doc; IMPROVEMENTS P8; docs/research/p8-e3.md; mlops/shadow_registry.json

Live (Phase 1 form). When one of a team's top-2 regular stars (by DK/min × mpg; appeared in ≥ 7 of the last 10 team games or one of the last 3) is absent in a sim's scenario, role-tier per-minute multipliers apply, fit on star-absence natural experiments in seasons < S. 2026-27 local bundle: other star DK/min ×1.057 (pts ×1.076, ast ×1.087, fta ×1.134, tov ×1.143), rotation ×1.035 (pts ×1.048, ast ×1.070). No per-minute shift when a third starter sits.

4. DFS engine

src/courtside/proj/dfs/ (optimizer.py, topn.py, runner.py, portfolio.py, field.py, xfactor.py, swap.py, swap_contest.py, showdown.py), sources/dfs.py; PLAYERS.md §5; IMPROVEMENTS §3

Scoring rules

DK Classic (sources/dfs.CLASSIC_SCORING): point 1, 3PM +0.5, rebound 1.25, assist 1.5, steal 2, block 2, turnover −0.5, double-double +1.5, triple-double +3 (a triple-double also gets the double-double bonus: +4.5 total; verified against FPPG and RotoGuru). Rosters PG/SG/SF/PF/C/G/F/UTIL, $50,000, ≥ 2 games and ≥ 2 teams; all rules read from the archived rules_json at build time. Showdown: CPT 1.5× salary and points + 5 UTIL, ≥ 2 teams, no late swap.

Solver, cash, Top N

  • Optimizer: OR-Tools CP-SAT, binary x[player, slot] over eligible slots; each slot group filled exactly; each player at most once; salary ≤ cap; ≥ min games and ≥ min teams via indicator variables; locks, bans, started-game players only if locked. Objective Σ score × multiplier × x with scores ×100 to integers; num_workers = 1, fixed seed (deterministic). Test oracle: pydfs-lineup-optimizer, equal optimal objective on 50 slates (and 33/33 Showdown slates).
  • Cash (live): maximise Σ (μi − 0.3·(μi − p25i))·xi (CASH_LAMBDA = 0.3). At lock−45, players with P(play) < 0.85 are excluded only if their game locks before the next run. The P(score ≥ cash line) objective from the plan is built OFF (CASH_PCASH_DEFAULT = "off"): on 281 RotoGuru slates it was −3.1 ± 2.6 pp vs the λ rule, because simulated and realised cash rates correlate only 0.03 within a slate.
  • Top N: k-best by no-good cuts Σi∈Lk xi ≤ 8 − d (d = min different, default 1; N default 10). Ranked in solve order (by mean).
  • Every mean used is μ* after the role blend.

Contest builder (GPP portfolio)

  1. Candidates: CP-SAT solves on a thread pool, deduplicated: scenario objectives 0.5·μi + 0.5·FPi,s for a random sim s; ownership-penalised μi − λ·owni, λ ~ U(0, 0.5·sd(μ)); noise objectives μi·exp(N(0, 0.12²)); the Top-N by mean; locked-player scenario solves for user minimums; leverage-tilted solves from xf-v2. Counts scale with N above 50.
  2. Scoring truth (truth_sims): truths,i = fps,i + 1[plays]·(β·(FPPGi − μi) + τ·sdi·zs,i), τ = 0.18 (residual sd exceeds sim sd by ~0.18 sd on harness rows). With +rb on, the β term is skipped (the blend already moved the mean).
  3. Field at the contest's archetype (below), scored per sim with our N entries occupying ranks jointly.
  4. Greedy selection: add the candidate with the largest marginal expected payout of the whole portfolio. Constraints: ≤ γ = 5 shared players with every chosen lineup (≥ #locks), exposure ≤ 60% from N ≥ 5, user min / max, locks in every lineup; refill solves under no-good cuts when the pool runs dry.
  5. Selection objective blend (OBJ_DEFAULT): the greedy scores a smoothed ladder where ranks in the top 1% pay (1 − 0.5)·prize + 0.5·mean top-1% prize; everything reported uses the real ladder. On 6 stored GPPs the held-out E[payout] was +0.5% ± 0.2 (N = 20) / +1.2% ± 0.3 (N = 150) with higher pick stability across sim halves.
  6. Sim split gs (SPLIT_DEFAULT): rows permuted; generation + selection on 55% (13,750 of 25,000), everything reported (E[payout], P(cash), P(top 1%), dupes, relative score, entries curve) on the disjoint 45%. Removes the winner's-curse inflation (OFF overstated E[payout] 6–12% on top-heavy GPPs); pick cost −1.0 ± 0.5% at N = 150.
  7. Output: per lineup P(cash), P(top 1%), E[payout], dupes; per portfolio E[payout] ± SE, P(any cash), relative score vs the Top-N-by-projection set, exposure table; entries curve for N = 1, 3, 5, 10, 20, 50, 150 on the same sims with SEs. Dollar E[payout] / ROI stay in a "details (simulated, uncalibrated)" toggle until ≥ 10 slates of uploaded standings.

Ownership model and modelled field

The ownership proxy (own-proxy-1.2) is a hand-set public-information utility, not a fitted model:

ui = conc · ( 1.10·z(valuei) + (0.70 + 0.70·small)·z(proji) + 0.20·z(totali) + 0.60·repli − 0.80·Qi − 0.60·benchi )
  • "Public projection" = 0.5·ours + 0.5·DK FPPG; value = public projection per $1k; total = team implied total; repl = our projected minutes ≥ 6 above recent; Q = DK status Q/GTD; bench = confirmed non-starter; small → 1 on a 2-game slate, 0 from 8 games.
  • Allocation: softmax per roster slot, balanced Sinkhorn-style so each slot column sums to its count (Σ own = 800% Classic), row cap 0.92 GPP / 0.97 cash; a salary shadow price λ (bisection) makes expected spend = 99.7% of the cap.
  • Archetypes (calibrated in direction on the 2026-10-20 standings, not fitted): cash conc 1.60, optimizer noise 0.03, optimizer share 0.40; small GPP 1.00 / 0.15 / 0.40; large GPP 0.90 / 0.18 / 0.20.

5. Props comparison (information only)

For each main prop market (points, rebounds, assists, PRA, 3PM) the Tonight card shows the line, our chance the stat finishes above it and the market's chance, side by side. Nothing is framed as advice.

  • Our chance: from the newest pre-tip run with projections for the game. With copula v1/v1r runs (live since 10-25) it is the empirical pmf of the player's played per-stat sims (PRA summed per sim) when he has ≥ 200 played sims; otherwise an approximation: minutes from the run's played-minutes quantiles, stat | minutes ~ NB1 with variance φ·mean (φ fit on the 3 seasons < S: pts 2.2–2.5, reb ~1.1, ast ~1.15, 3PM ~1.05).   • Lines and pushes: half line L → P(Y ≥ ⌈L⌉). Integer line → P(Y > L), P(Y = L), P(Y < L); compared on the market's no-push basis P(Y > L)/(1 − P(Y = L)).
  • Market's chance: proportional (multiplicative) no-vig, p = qabove/(qabove + qbelow), q = implied chance of the American price; hold = qabove + qbelow − 1. A read-only check on 16,311 game closes found power de-vig worse (+0.0007 ± 0.0003 LL).
  • Props are never features of the projection (rule L6). F9 (prop lines as features) is built and OFF pending an owner amendment to L6.

G3, the pre-registered blend test (frozen; the evaluator pins the file's sha256).

  • Question: out of sample, does logit pblend = a·logit(m) + b·logit(o) (no intercept, m, o clipped to [0.001, 0.999]) predict "finishes above the line" with lower log loss than the de-vigged market m alone?
  • Rows: (game, player, stat) at the lock−45 state: the newest ok lock45 run before tip, market state at that run's created_at; DraftKings provider; pushes, voids, hold outside [0, 0.25] excluded.
  • Fit: walk-forward per slate on all earlier graded rows, ≥ 1,000 training rows.
  • Primary metric: mean[LL(blend) − LL(m)], SE two-way game × player; PASS if z ≤ −2.0 on the first 60 scored slates; any stat at z ≥ +2 → owner review. No decision before 60.
  • Timeline: 10-20 … 10-24 are training only; scoring starts 2026-10-25 (amended because +c1r changes the props source from the approximation to sims). Decision around late December. A pass only allows proposing the blended chance as information.
  • Interim, descriptive, in sample (1,671 rows over the 4 pre-registration slates): LL market 0.6882 vs ours 0.7082 (ours − market +0.0200, z +3.2; reb and PRA worst); fitted a 0.73 (0.24), b 0.21 (0.08). The ledger holds 2,096 rows through 10-24.

Live shadows (2026-27 is the confirmatory set)

  • After every pregame job, the day's Classic slate with the most games still to tip is projected by the champion once and then by each active arm (champion + one switch, via the pipeline setter), same seed, 10,000 sims, read-only DB; output only to <data>/proj/shadows/<day>/ (p_play, E[min], E[min | plays], E[DK], 100 mid-point DK quantiles). Never served.
  • Guards: skip if any lock / swap window falls in [now − 10 min, now + budget + 2 min]; 240 s budget (SIGALRM per arm); an arm whose bundle is missing is skipped (a shadow never fits a bundle).
  • Grading (postgame shadow_grade): arm − champion on identical rows, DNP = 0, accumulated over the season at the registered run slot; SE two-way night × player. Decision (shadows.decide): nothing before 14 nights; "lower" arms pass at z ≤ −2 with no tier z > +2 and guards held, fail at z ≥ +2 or 60 nights; "not worse" arms pass at z ≤ +2. Repeated nightly looks make a pass easier than a single test, so a pass is the condition for asking the owner, not the flip itself.
  • Registry today: active +gd (P(play) LL at 10:00, not worse), +lf (last run, 21 nights), +tm (not worse), +rs (minutes MAE on changed rows, ≥ 100 rows), +us (21 nights); shipped +tr, +ep (window to 11-19), +pq. As of 2026-10-24: 1 graded night, all pending.

Real-results calibration from uploaded standings

  • Uploads: the owner's DK contest history and standings CSVs (rank, points, lineup, %Drafted per roster position). courtside dfs real-calib runs after each postgame job and fills the "Real contests vs our chances" card on /health/dfs.
  • Unit = slate (all lineups of a night share one draw). Double-ups: percentile of (lineup score − real cash line) within its simulated distribution, which detects a 0.10 shift in ~33 slates; a plain cash rate needs ~45. Top-1%: needs 100+ slates, so mean finishing percentile is tracked instead. Ownership: per slate MAE, rank correlation and top-10 bias by contest type and slate size.
  • Nightly, without standings: ours vs an FPPG-optimizer baseline (paired), share of the hindsight lineup, tail calibration by tier.
  • Gates tied to it: ownership refit, dollar E[payout] / ROI headlines and P(cash) on the slate page all wait for ≥ 10 uploaded slates.

The ship process

  1. Build the change behind an env switch whose *_DEFAULT is OFF, with its own bundle suffix if it changes fitted parameters and a version tag either way.
  2. Prove OFF is bit-identical: sha1 over every sim array and the players frame (Linux goldens; macOS rounds differently), on historical days and real slates.
  3. Evaluate paired with common random numbers on the harness, the live-like slice and RotoGuru; record hypothesis, metric, SEs (one- and two-way), sets, attempts and decision in docs/research/switch-ledger.md.
  4. Optionally register a live shadow with a pre-registered bar (bars change only before the first graded night).
  5. The owner approves; pre-fit bundles are copied to prod var/proj/ before flipping the constant (so a switch-on never fits at lock time); the env variable remains as the rollback. Tests pin switches off in tests/conftest.py.

8. Known weaknesses and what's next

Weaknesses

  • Minutes and availability dominate the error. 39–57% of the played DK variance is minutes (c1r diagnosis). Lock−45 vs oracle costs +0.22 DK MAE; most of the live value-ranking gap to FPPG is availability; the opener's real X-factor nights (Drummond 43 DK at 0.3%, Green 26.5 at 0.4%) were minutes / P(play) misses, not tail misses. Planned demotions still carry P(play) +0.06–0.08 on demoted bench rows with E[min] ≥ 10 after +mf.
  • Star under-projection. Star tier bias −1.1 to −1.4 DK before P+F2, −0.6 to −0.9 after; +mf moved it to about −0.19 / −0.09 in the harness; live slice star bias −0.52 at lock−45. Stars in big wins run 2.0–2.6 DK low at the right minutes (a per-minute blowout effect), and F3 would add 0.06–0.10 DK more under-projection.
  • Early-season data. October rates are mostly last season's plus preseason (+ep); promoted / new players get low per-minute rates (pre-reconciliation team points 7–15 below the market). Live-like baselines come from Dec–Apr nights, so October may run worse.
  • F3 tracking was not collected for 2026-27 until today, so the F3+F6 extension could not run live and has no live evidence; its historical gain was 1.3 / 3.0 SE end to end.
  • Ownership is a hand-set proxy: real double-up fields are far more concentrated (30–35% of entries on one lineup), the misses are rising-role young players and stars back from layoffs (Harper 15% proxy vs 66–93% real). Only 3 slates of standings.
  • Evaluation reuse: about 20 decisions tuned on the same seasons; c1r's shape choices were made looking at the harness. 2026-27 live is the confirmatory set.
  • Game model: no live 2026-27 metric documented; P(play) inside the game model is still the hand-set map (B4); trained on the close but priced on earlier lines.

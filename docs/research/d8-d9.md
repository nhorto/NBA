## 1. D9: SEs on the entries curve (display only)

- Each curve point now has `se`: binomial SEs for P(any cash) / P(top 1 %) / P(top 0.1 %) /
  P(1st) (`sqrt(p(1-p)/S)`, exact for iid sim rows), and sd(per-sim total payout)/sqrt(S) for
  E[payout] (and per dollar). The numbers themselves are unchanged.
- The page shows a small "± 0.4 pt" under each chance from 1 % up, and "± 35%" (relative)
  below 1 %. Older stored builds get the binomial SE from p and n_sims. A note says it is
  simulation noise only (not projection or field error). The CLI prints the same.
- Example: P(1st) of 1 in 25,000 on 25,000 sims is ± 100 %; 1 in 2,500 is ± 32 %. The rare
  columns are rough.
- The SEs do not cover the picking: points above the build's N are picked on the first 8,000
  sims and scored on all of them (with the split ON, the report rows are disjoint from the
  picks).

## 2. D9: selection objective (switch `COURTSIDE_PORTFOLIO_OBJ`, OFF)

Values `flat` / `blend` (tags `+obj-flat` / `+obj-blend` on the portfolio version). The greedy
(and the curve's picks) score a smoothed ladder; every reported number uses the real one. Both
keep the ladder's total money:
- `flat`: the top 0.1 % of ranks (max(1, ceil(0.001 M))) all pay the mean of their prizes.
- `blend`: ranks in the top 1 % pay 0.5 x prize + 0.5 x the mean top-1 % prize (E[payout]
  blended with P(top 1 %) priced at the mean top-1 % prize).

**Test** (`research/d9_stability.py`). Per contest and replicate: the shipped inputs (medium
field, truth sims), sim rows shuffled into G (2,500, candidate objectives), A and B (two disjoint
selection halves, 7,500 each) and C (held out, 7,500). One candidate pool per replicate (built
on G), scored on A, B and C with the build's dupe model. Each objective picks N on A and N on B.
Stability = lineup overlap |pick_A ∩ pick_B| / N; quality = joint E[payout] of the picks on C
(real ladder), paired against OFF on the same rows. The candidate pool is fixed within a
replicate, so this isolates selection noise. 10 replicates at N = 20, 5 at N = 150.

**Read:**
- Both raise stability. `flat` helps most where P(1st) is tiny (the 44k field: overlap 0.36 ->
  0.68 and +2.5 % E[payout]) but costs 1-2 % on the 7.8k-14k fields, where the flattened band is
  only 8-15 ranks and the objective stops paying for 1st.
- At N = 150 both help everywhere (flat +2.3 %, blend +1.2 %, all 6 contests up): with 150 picks
  the noisy 1st-place sims steer many marginal picks.

  (N = 20, 5 of 6 contests up) and +1.2 % ± 0.3 (N = 150, 6 of 6).

**Recommendation (D9 objective):** ship `blend` (owner OK needed; not flipped). It raises the
overlap at both sizes (+0.06 at N = 20, +0.08 at N = 150) and adds held-out E[payout] in 11 of 12
contest x N cells (pooled +0.5 % ± 0.2 and +1.2 % ± 0.3; the worst cell -0.1 %). `flat` is
stronger at N = 150 and on huge fields (+7 % on the 44k field) but costs 1-2 % at N = 20 on
7.8k-14k fields, so keep it OFF. Caveat: sims only (held-out rows of the same model); the field
is the proxy.

## 3. D8: contest-aware late swap (switch `COURTSIDE_SWAP_CONTEST`, OFF)

OFF, `swap.plan` re-solves each entry's open slots by its mean (cash: mu - 0.3 (mu - p25)) and
changes it when the mean gains 0.5 points. That strips the leverage and the spread a
contest-builder set was built with. ON (`swap_contest.py`):
- **Sims:** the projection run's stored sims, scored like the builder (`truth_sims`), with:
  - known Outs (DK O, late news to Out): only the sim rows where the run drew him out (P10
    scenario arrays); skipped if fewer than 1,000 rows remain or the run predates P10; his
    column is zeroed either way;
  - locked players at their realised points when given (`locked_points`). **Live there is no
    in-game feed, so locked players keep their sims.** The replay passes final box scores.
- **Field:** the contest's medium field proxy (80 optimizer solves), same rows.
- **Candidates:** the mean solve (what OFF picks) + 16 scenario solves (0.5 mean + 0.5 one
  sim row) under the same locks, bans, cuts and caps, + the entry as it stands. Later entries
  are cut against earlier entries' mean solves, as OFF cuts them against finals.
- **Choice:** contest by contest, two coordinate passes: GPP by the builder's greedy marginal
  (joint E[payout] of all our entries in the contest); cash by P(cash) (a ladder paying 1 to every
  paid place). An entry that can stay (nobody ruled out or started in an open slot) changes only
  for a gain above 1 paired SE.
- **Contest:** the entry's Contest ID (uploaded CSV); saved lineups have none, so they use the
  slate's largest captured double-up (cash) / GPP (Top N), else presets.
- `plan(..., run_id=...)` can pin the projection run; `swap.load_projections` takes it too.
- The runner's automatic swap (`resolve_targets`, saved cash / Top N) is unchanged.
- Runtime ON: 6-45 s for 31 entries (OFF 0.1-2 s). ON has its own 120 s budget, which goes
  past the 30 s §5.8 budget.

**Replay** (`research/d8_replay.py`). Seven stored late-swap nights (swap time T = the stored
swap run's time; 155547 is the 10-23 run that skipped with "no late news touches a saved
lineup"). Entries: a 20-entry builder portfolio for the night's main GPP (built on the last
pre-lock run, shipped settings; prod has no stored builder portfolios), plus the site's saved
lineups as they stood at T (cash + Top N 10). Projection run = the newest run with sims at or
before T. Locked players = their graded actual DK points. Sim rows split at random: A (ON picks
on these), B (held out). Arms: keep (no swap), mean (OFF), ca (ON). Scored on B with the same
conditioning and a fresh field / truth seed; GPP groups by joint E[payout], cash by P(cash).
3 seeds per night (portfolio seed, row split). ± = SE across B rows / across seeds.

**A first replay run had a bug** (kept as `out/d8_replay_v0_bug.json`): ON solved every target

the rerun.

**Caveats:**
- Held-out sims, not real results. ON optimises the model's own objective, so the gain is
  "out of sample in the sims"; the real check is real standings of swapped entries.
- Locked points are final box scores (the replay knows them; live does not). Live ON conditions
  only on known Outs.
- Out conditioning ran on the two nights with P10 arrays (155547, 155553: ~10 % of rows
  dropped). A player Out now but never drawn out in the sims (0 rows) cannot be conditioned on:
  his column is just zeroed. That is the surprise-Out case where it would matter most.
- The builder entries are simulated owner sets; the owner hand-enters lineups and the swap job
  only sees lineups saved on the site. ON only helps entries uploaded in a DK entries CSV (with
  contest ids) or the saved lineups.

**Recommendation (D8):** ship ON for the owner-facing swap (owner OK needed; not flipped),
after moving the page's plan off the web request (ON takes up to 45 s). On every night with open
entries the contest-aware choice beat the mean re-solve in held-out sims (builder +9.8 %, Top N
+11 %), and it changes about half as many entries. Keep checking real standings of swapped
entries; live it conditions on known Outs only.

## 2. P4: weighted reconciliation

### 2.1 Design

Today one factor c per team scales every player's points so that the team sums to the implied
total. ON, the split comes from Gaussian conditioning on the team sum:

- Player i's expected points e_i have a team-common relative error (variance theta e_i², the
  market's pace and efficiency news) and his own rate error V_i = e_i² / (r_i (n_i + kappa)).
  r_i is the EB points rate, n_i its evidence minutes and kappa the prior's pseudo-minutes.
- delta_i = gap × (theta e_i e + V_i) / (theta e² + Σ V), e = Σ e_i. theta = 0 is the pure
  uncertainty split; theta → ∞ is today's uniform c.
- c_i = 1 + delta_i / e_i, clipped to [0.5, 2.0]. The rest goes to the players not at a bound.
  Any remainder gets one uniform factor in [0.75, 1.33]. Other DK stats move by c_i^alpha.
- theta per season S: the grid value with the least squared error of e_i c_i vs actual points,
  on captured team-games of seasons < S (`p4_p6_eval.py rwfit`). Captures: avail 2018-19,
  2019-20, 2020-21, 2023-24, 2024-25 and live 2025-26 (OFF runs, same arm).
- A pre-reconciliation |gap| > GAP_CHECK (6) is logged as a projection error with the three
  players that absorbed most of it. The `proj_recon_gap` data-check detail carries them too.

### 2.2 The fit says "keep it proportional"

Squared error per player row of the reconciled mean vs actual points (lower is better):

| capture | theta 0 | 1e-4 | 1e-3 | 1e-2 | uniform |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| avail 2018-19 | 25.199 | 25.177 | 25.132 | 25.119 | 25.126 |
| avail 2019-20 | 25.658 | 25.647 | 25.625 | 25.622 | 25.616 |
| avail 2020-21 | 24.353 | 24.330 | 24.282 | 24.267 | 24.266 |
| avail 2023-24 | 24.237 | 24.225 | 24.202 | 24.195 | 24.194 |
| avail 2024-25 | 24.347 | 24.326 | 24.278 | 24.259 | 24.259 |
| live 2025-26 | 24.279 | 24.254 | 24.210 | 24.197 | 24.194 |

- Error falls toward the uniform split on every capture. Walk-forward theta: 0.01 for 2019-20
  to 2023-24 (about uniform), uniform (1e6) for 2024-25 on.
- A cross-check splits the gap by e_i^beta (beta = 1 is uniform). The best beta is 1.0-1.5 on
  every capture; beta < 1 (toward small, uncertain players) is worse everywhere.
- So the market's gap to our sum is about the team (pace, efficiency, a missing name), not about
  which of our rates are thin.
- The gap is large and common: sd 6.2-6.9 points per team-game in the harness, and |gap| > 6
  in 30-36 % of team-games. With GAP_CHECK = 6 the new log fires on about a third of team-games
  (630-880 per harness season). As an error alarm, a threshold near 2.5 sd (~15) would fit
  better. That is an owner choice; GAP_CHECK is unchanged.

## 3. P6: player-specific minutes spread

### 3.1 Design

Step 6's noise is per tier: minutes = (plan + g(margin)) × exp(mu_t + sigma_t xi).

- ON, each player gets his own lower and upper scale: sigma_t s_lo when xi < 0, sigma_t s_hi
  when xi ≥ 0. This is a two-piece normal in log minutes.
- It is re-centred so that E[exp(noise)] stays the tier's. The GBM sets the mean; the spread
  model only sets the shape.
- s_lo = (q50 − q10) / z90 and s_hi = (q90 − q50) / z90 come from LightGBM quantile models
  (q10, q50, q90) of log(actual / (plan + g(margin))). Rows: played, plan ≥ 5.
- Features: the minutes GBM's own (volatility `min_sd` / `ew_vf`, fouls `pf36` / `last_pf`,
  return from injury `missed_now` / `gsr_now` / `abs_len_now`, `new_team`, the scenario
  features), plus the plan (log plan, plan vs the fast / slow EWMA).
- Each scale is divided by its tier's mean on the training rows and clipped to [0.4, 2.5].
- Fit (`minutes.fit_spread`, on the 5 seasons < S): out-of-fold plans exactly as `fit_v1`
  makes them, then quantile models out of fold by season (for the references and the
  calibration), then refit on all rows. Last, mu / sigma per tier are recalibrated by
  simulation with the spread on (the +mf calibration). Nothing else of the arm changes.
- Same random draws as OFF (common random numbers); the DNP draws are identical.
- Cost: about 3 minutes per season to fit; harness runtime unchanged within noise.

# E4 / E5: DFS-relevant evaluation and the real-standings report

*2026-10-24. IMPROVEMENTS.md E4 and E5. Report-only work: no live number, lineup or shadow bar
changes. Data: prod DB read-only (live grades 2026-10-20 .. 10-23; the owner's entries and the
four 10-20 standings files). Runs on the Mac scratch dir `~/Developer/courtside-compute/e4e5/`
(code tree = HEAD + these files; JSON in `out/`).*

## Plain-language summary

- **Value ranking (E4).** The new metric asks: after taking out what the salary already says,
  does the projection rank players' extra value right? On the four live nights, on DFS-relevant
  players (salary $4k+ or projected 15+), our projection's value correlation is 0.23 vs 0.10
  for DK FPPG (difference +0.13, SE 0.06 clustered by game). Most of that gap is availability:
  players we expected to sit or play little whom FPPG still rated. Among players we projected
  for 10+ minutes the gap is +0.05 (SE 0.07): not separable from noise yet.
- **Real contests (E5).** Two slates have real entries. On 10-20 (3 games, four standings
  files) our chances read a bit high in the GPP and about right in the double-ups. On 10-23
  (6 games, five GPPs, history only) our Top-10 finished better than our simulations expected:
  22 of 27 entries in the money vs about 65 % simulated. Two slates are one draw each; nothing
  is calibrated yet.
- **Every night, no upload needed:** over 10 Classic slates our in-effect cash lineup beat the
  DK-FPPG optimizer baseline by 47 points (SE 18) and the Top N by 42 (SE 16); our Top N reached
  0.72 of the best possible lineup, FPPG 0.61. The FPPG baseline is weak on nights with late
  scratches (it scored 127 and 165 on two slates), so this gap is partly availability too.
- **One fix found on the way:** `field.calibration_report` (`courtside dfs field-calib`) passed
  its arguments to the ownership proxy in the wrong order, so its "proxy vs real ownership"
  columns were always missing. Fixed (report only).

## E4. What was built

| piece | where |
|---|---|
| `dfs_population(salary, *proj)`: salary >= $4k or any compared projection >= 15 DK | `proj/metrics.py` |
| `salary_implied(y, salary, slate)`: per-slate OLS of actual DK on salary | `proj/metrics.py` |
| `corr_cluster`, `corr_paired_cluster`: Pearson r and r_a - r_b with a clustered SE (influence function, delta method) | `proj/metrics.py` |
| `value_ranking(preds, y, salary, slate, clusters, base=)` | `proj/metrics.py` |
| harness: `player_report()["dfs_pop"]` (MAE, gain vs FPPG proxy, CRPS vs naive, value metric on RotoGuru rows) and the T2.4 slice's `V` population (`dk_mae_dfs`, `dk_crps_dfs`, `cov80_dfs` in `rep["dfs_pop"]`) | `proj/backtest.py` |
| live grades: `courtside proj value-eval` | `proj/value_eval.py` |
| shadows: `arm_report()["reported_only"]` (paired CRPS on the DFS rows and the value metric, arm vs champion); no bar or decision uses it | `proj/shadows.py` |

Definitions:
- **Population.** A paired comparison passes every compared forecast to `dfs_population`, so
  neither side is selected on its own numbers (ours >= 15 OR FPPG >= 15 OR salary >= $4k). On
  live grades, players DK listed OUT at the run's capture are removed (nobody rosters them; the
  grader's baselines drop them too).
- **Salary-implied.** Fit per slate on the actual points of the rows being compared (hindsight),
  so it is the same line for every forecast.
- **SE.** Date clusters as asked. With 4 nights a date-clustered SE is not usable (4 clusters
  give SEs of 0.01 next to game-clustered 0.06); the game-clustered SE is reported alongside and
  is the one to read until there are ~20 nights.
- **Live rows.** One row per (game, player) on DK Classic slates: main slate first, then lock10
  > lock45 > swap; manual runs only on nights with no scheduled run.

Shadow rows gain three report-only columns (`dk_c`, `dk_a`, `salary`). A night cached before
this change is upgraded by recomputing it and merging only the new columns, so every existing
cached number stays as it was (test). The prod shadows have no graded nights yet (first run
10-24).

## E5. What was built

- `proj/dfs/real_calib.py`, command `courtside dfs real-calib [--sims 2000] [--slate S]
  [--no-write] [--json] [--db PATH]`. Writes `<data>/reports/real_calib/real_calib.json` (and a
  dated copy). Reads the DB read-only. 24 s on the Mac with field sims for two slates.
- Card "Real contests vs our chances" on `/health/dfs` (display only, reads that JSON; hidden
  until the command has run).
- `standings.import_history`: slate inference. `Contest_Date_EST` is Eastern wall time
  ("2026-10-23 19:00:00" = 23:00Z; "2027-01-06 19:00:00" = 00:00Z next day). When exactly one
  slate of the row's game type (Classic 70, Showdown 81) has that nba_day and lock_utc, the
  entries get that slate_id. Two slates at the same lock (e.g. two showdowns) leave it empty. A
  contest's captured slate wins. No contest row is created, so the grader's reference contests
  do not change. On the real history it gives 154575 for 10-20 and 155547 for 10-23 (the slates
  linked by hand). The upload's `detail.contests` now keeps places paid, prize pool, entries, fee
  and date per contest; older uploads are read from the archived file.
- `field.calibration_report`: the argument-order fix above.

What the report contains per slate (unit = slate, as E5 asks):
- **Contests and lines.** Type, field, places paid (payout table, DK's double-up rule, or the
  history's Places_Paid). Real cash and top-1 % lines: exact from standings, else bracketed by
  the owner's entries (best entry that did not cash, worst that did).
- **Entries.** Place, share of the field beaten, in the money, the lineup (lineup_id, player
  set, or the one stored lineup that scored exactly those points; ties go to the run that
  explains most entries; on 10-23 all ten entries map to manual run da4151b16eb6 #1-10).
- **With `--sims`.** Each entered lineup against a modelled field of that contest (contest
  builder field, calibrated truth): simulated P(cash), the PIT of (score - real cash line) in the
  lineup's simulated (score - simulated line) (an interval when the line is bracketed), and the
  PIT of the real place among simulated places. Calibrated chances give PITs averaging 0.5.
- **Ownership** (standings only): MAE, rank correlation, top-10 bias per contest, grouped by
  contest type and slate size (2-4 games vs 5+).
- **Nightly:** our in-effect cash lineup and Top N vs the DK-FPPG optimizer, and shares of the
  hindsight-best lineup, paired by slate.

Not in this report (they live elsewhere): β drift (P1) and tail calibration by tier (D6).

## What needs the owner

1. **One big-GPP standings file per slate.** For 10-23 the most useful is the $35K mini-MAX
   (197041978, 83,234 entries: biggest field, exact cash and top-1 % lines, real ownership on a
   6-game slate, the first 5+ game ownership data). The $40K Sharpshooter (15,854) is a smaller
   file with a captured payout table. Without standings the 10-23 lines stay bracketed and there
   is no ownership check.
2. **Double-ups.** DU P(cash) calibration (the E5 headline test) needs double-up entries; 10-23
   had none. One DU per slate, plus its standings, is the cheapest way to accumulate it.
3. **Running the report.** It is not in the nightly job. After each upload run
   `courtside dfs real-calib --sims 2000` on the Mac (about 25 s); the /health/dfs card shows the
   last run. Adding it to the postgame job is a one-line change if wanted.
4. **Contest history uploads** now link to their slate automatically when the time matches one
   Classic slate; nothing else needs to be linked by hand.
